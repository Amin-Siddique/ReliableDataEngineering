Have a question, feedback, or want to collaborate? Here is how to reach me.

## Get in Touch

- **Email:** [amin.siddique@outlook.com](mailto:amin.siddique@outlook.com)
- **Medium:** [medium.com/@amin-siddique](https://medium.com/@amin-siddique)

## What I Respond To

I read every message and prioritize the following:

- **Technical questions** about articles on this site -- if something is unclear or you want to go deeper on a topic, I am happy to help
- **Corrections or suggestions** for published content -- accuracy matters and I appreciate readers who catch mistakes
- **Collaboration opportunities** related to data engineering, AI tools, or developer productivity
- **Guest post proposals** on topics like data engineering, AI agents, pipeline design, or developer tooling -- include a brief outline and I will get back to you
- **Speaking or podcast invitations** related to data engineering, AI in the data stack, or building reliable systems

## What I Cannot Help With

To set expectations, I generally cannot assist with:

- Debugging your specific pipeline or codebase (I recommend Stack Overflow or relevant community forums for that)
- Vendor-specific support requests (reach out to the vendor directly)
- Requests for paid endorsements or sponsored content (I do not publish sponsored posts)

## Response Time

I aim to respond within a few business days. If your message is about a factual error in a published article, I will prioritize it. Complex technical discussions may take longer as I want to give thoughtful responses rather than quick ones.
